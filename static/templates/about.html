ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is NutriAI?

Making Nutrition Smarter

NutriAI is a smart web-based nutrition management system that combines Artificial Intelligence with health and nutrition analysis.

Users can upload a food image and the AI model detects the food item and provides estimated nutritional information such as calories, protein, carbohydrates and fats.

NutriAI also provides BMI calculation, personalized diet recommendations, healthy recipes and health progress tracking.
